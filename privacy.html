Overview

Office Attendance Tracker ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that helps individuals and teams track daily work attendance — office, work from home, holiday, and leave statuses.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data. By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy.

✓ Short version: Your attendance data belongs to you. We never sell it, never share it with third parties for advertising, and you can delete it at any time.
02

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the App:

  • Google Account Identity: Your name, email address, profile picture URL, and Google account ID — provided when you sign in with Google.
  • Profile Information: Mobile number, company name, and office working hours that you voluntarily enter in the App.
  • Attendance Records: Daily attendance status entries (Office, Work From Home, Holiday, Leave) that you mark in the calendar.
  • Location Data (optional): If you opt in to Smart Location features, your office and home GPS coordinates and geofence radius. Stored only in your own Google Sheet, never transmitted to any third-party server.
  • Device Data: We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or analytics data.
⚠ Location is always optional. The App never requests location in the background. Location is only read on-demand when you tap "Use current location" or when the App is open and you tap a notification.
03

Google Sign-In & OAuth

The App uses Google Identity Services (GIS) for authentication. When you sign in:

  • Google provides a signed ID token containing your name, email, profile picture, and unique Google account ID.
  • We use the ID token solely to identify you within the App and to securely authenticate requests to our backend (Google Apps Script).
  • We do not access your Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, or any other Google service beyond what is listed below.
  • Your Google credentials (password) are never seen or stored by the App.

The App requests the following OAuth scopes:

  • openid — Confirms your identity via Google.
  • email — Reads your Google email address for account identification.
  • profile — Reads your display name and profile picture URL.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — Creates and manages only the specific spreadsheet created by the App in your Google Drive (optional personal sheet feature — see Section 4).
04

Google Drive Access (drive.file scope)

The drive.file scope is requested only if you choose to connect a personal attendance spreadsheet to your own Google Drive. This is an optional feature.

With this scope, the App can:

  • Create a single Google Spreadsheet in your Drive named "Attendance - [Your Name]".
  • Read and write attendance rows to that specific file only.

With this scope, the App cannot:

  • Access any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Delete files outside the App-created spreadsheet.
  • Share your files with anyone else.
✓ You can revoke Drive access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access does not delete your spreadsheet — it only disconnects the App from it.
05

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected exclusively to provide App functionality:

  • Authenticating your identity and maintaining your session across devices.
  • Saving and retrieving your attendance records from a shared Google Sheet (accessible only to you and the App administrator).
  • Displaying your profile information, name, and photo within the App.
  • Sending attendance reminder push notifications at your configured office start and end times (only when you grant notification permission).
  • Checking proximity to your configured office or home location to prompt attendance marking (only when the App is open or a notification is tapped).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, machine learning training, or any purpose other than operating the App.

06

Data Storage & Security

Where is data stored?

  • Attendance & Profile data is stored in a Google Spreadsheet managed via Google Apps Script, hosted entirely within Google's infrastructure.
  • Optional personal sheet data is stored in a Google Spreadsheet in your own Google Drive account.
  • Session tokens (Google ID tokens) are stored in your browser's localStorage and are never transmitted to any server other than Google.
  • Location coordinates (if set) are stored only in your profile row in Google Sheets and never on a third-party server.

Security measures:

  • All communication between the App and Google APIs is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Every request to the App's backend (Google Apps Script) includes a Google ID token that is verified server-side.
  • No passwords or sensitive credentials are stored anywhere in the App.
⚠ The App uses a shared Google Sheet as its main data store. The app administrator (the person who deployed this App) has owner access to that sheet. For fully private storage, use the optional personal Google Drive sheet feature.
07

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for commercial purposes.

Data may be accessible to:

  • Google LLC — as the underlying platform providing authentication (Google Identity Services), data storage (Google Sheets and Drive), and hosting infrastructure.
  • The App Administrator — the individual who deployed and maintains this App instance has access to the main Google Sheet containing attendance records.

No other parties have access to your data. We do not integrate with advertising networks, analytics platforms, or any other external services.

08

Your Rights & Controls

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: All your attendance data is visible to you in the App calendar at any time.
  • Edit: You can update or reset any attendance entry at any time.
  • Delete: Contact the App administrator to request deletion of your profile and attendance records from the main sheet. Your personal Drive sheet can be deleted directly from Google Drive.
  • Revoke Google access: Visit myaccount.google.com/permissions to revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time.
  • Notification opt-out: Revoke notification permission from your browser or device settings at any time.
  • Location opt-out: Location settings are entirely optional and can be cleared from My Profile at any time.
09

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for working professionals and is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.
