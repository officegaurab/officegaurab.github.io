What is this app?

Attendance tracking, without the complexity

Office Attendance Tracker is a free PWA (installable on Android & iOS) that lets you keep a private calendar of where you worked each day. No spreadsheet expertise required.

Mark any day with one of these statuses:

✓ Office ✓ Work From Home Holiday Leave No Show + Office No Show + Home
🔒

Your data stays yours

Sign in with Google — your attendance is stored in Google Sheets that only you and the app admin can access. Optionally connect your own private Google Drive spreadsheet for fully personal storage. We never sell or share your data. Read our full Privacy Policy →

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built for individuals who need a simple, reliable way to track and recall their work location each day.

📅

Visual Calendar

Colour-coded monthly calendar makes it instant to see your attendance pattern at a glance. Navigate across months freely.

🔔

Smart Reminders

Get a push notification at your office start time reminding you to mark attendance. A second reminder fires in the afternoon if you still haven't marked.

📍

Location Aware

Optionally set your office and home locations. When you arrive, the app prompts you to mark attendance automatically — no manual tapping needed.

📊

Attendance Stats

Monthly, quarterly, and annual summaries show your office days, WFH days, leaves, and office target percentage at a glance.

☁️

Cross-Device Sync

Sign in with the same Google account on any phone, tablet, or computer and all your data syncs instantly via Google Sheets.

📤

CSV & Excel Export

Download your full attendance history as a clean CSV file at any time for your own records or to submit to HR.

📱

Installable PWA

Install directly to your Android or iOS home screen. Works offline. No app store required. Free forever.

🔑

Google Sign-In Only

No passwords to remember. Sign in securely with your existing Google account. Your credentials are never seen by this app.
